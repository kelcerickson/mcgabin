The letter found inside the walls during renovation OUR STORY A home loved into existence. Twice. Before we ever picked up a hammer, someone else loved this place. The original builder — an artist — left a letter hidden inside the walls of this cabin on McGinnis Street. We didn't know it was there until our family started the renovation, kids and all, swinging hammers and tearing down walls together. And there it was. "May you feel love in every piece of wood, every nail, every drop of paint and inch of concrete." He wrote to the house itself. He signed it: your humble custodian, and grateful resident. We've tried to honor that. Every wall we rebuilt, every room we finished — we put the same intention into it that he did. McGabin isn't just a place to stay.
